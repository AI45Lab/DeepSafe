SALAD-Bench

SALAD-Bench 建立了一个分层级、多用途的安全评估框架，通过一个基于三级分类体系构建的大规模数据集，严格评估大型语言模型、攻击策略及防御机制。SALAD-Bench constitutes a hierarchical, multi-purpose safety evaluation framework that rigorously assesses Large Language Models, attack strategies, and defense mechanisms through a large-scale dataset structured across a three-level taxonomy.

Introduction

评测集亮点

简介

SALAD-Bench 解决了现有安全基准测试的关键局限性，包括性能饱和、多样性不足以及缺乏对对抗性场景的支持等问题。

该框架利用包含 6 个领域、16 项任务和 65 个具体类别的综合三级分类体系，系统地对安全挑战进行分类。

它集成了一个包含超过 21,000 个样本的数据集，涵盖标准查询、增强攻击提示词和多项选择题，并结合了 MD-Judge 等专用自动化评估器以确保高精度的安全评估。

此外，SALAD-Bench 展示了当代大型语言模型在应对复杂新兴威胁时的不同韧性，并验证了多种防御策略的有效性，为未来的安全对齐研究提供了强有力的标准。

侧重点

模型输出内容的安全性评估、攻击增强评估、防御评估

解决的主要问题

解决现有安全基准分类粗糙、缺乏攻击/防御针对性以及评估器不可靠的问题

Introduction

评测方法与指标

Evaluator：Mdjudge-0.1

含义：用于对模型输出结果进行安全性判定与量化统计的评估器。

用法：该评估器负责判断模型在给定测试样本下的输出是否符合安全规范，并为后续安全率 攻击成功率(ASR) 等指标的计算提供统一、可复现的判定口径。

Metrics：安全率

在一组测试样本里，模型的输出被评为“安全/合规”（没有按要求生成有害内容、没有越狱成功、遵守安全策略）的比例

数值越高说明模型越安全、更不容易产出违规内容

Metrics：攻击成功率(ASR)

在攻击/越狱提示下，模型被诱导生成了“有害/违规内容”（或绕过安全限制）的比例

数值越高说明攻击越有效、模型越脆弱（通常 ASR 与安全率呈反向关系）。

Highlight of the Evaluation Set

Introduction

SALAD-Bench addresses the critical limitations of existing safety benchmarks, which often suffer from performance saturation, insufficient diversity, and a lack of support for adversarial scenarios.

The framework utilizes a comprehensive three-level taxonomy comprising 6 domains, 16 tasks, and 65 distinct categories to systematically categorize safety challenges.

It integrates a dataset of over 21,000 samples, encompassing standard queries, attack-enhanced prompts, and multiple-choice questions, alongside specialized automated evaluators like MD-Judge to ensure high-precision safety assessment.

Furthermore, SALAD-Bench demonstrates the varying resilience of contemporary LLMs against complex emerging threats and validates the efficacy of diverse defense tactics, providing a robust standard for future safety alignment research.

Focus

Safety evaluation, attack enhancement assessment, and defense evaluation

Solved Problems

Addressing the coarse classification of existing benchmarks, the lack of targeted attack/defense mechanisms, and the unreliability of evaluators.

Introduction

Evaluation Methods and Metrics

Evaluator：Mdjudge-0.1

Meaning: An evaluator used for safety judgment and quantitative statistics of model output results.

Usage: This evaluator is responsible for judging whether the model's output under given test samples complies with safety standards, and provides a unified and reproducible judgment standard for the calculation of subsequent metrics such as Safety rate Attack success rate (ASR).

Metrics：Safety rate

The proportion of the model's output rated as "safe/compliant" (no harmful content was generated as required, no jailbreak was successful, security policy adherence) was rated in a set of test samples

The higher the value, the safer the model is and the less likely it is to produce illegal content

Metrics：Attack success rate (ASR)

The proportion of models being induced to generate "harmful/violating content" (or bypassing security restrictions) at the attack/jailbreak prompt

Higher values indicate that the attack is more effective and the model is more vulnerable (ASR is usually inversely related to safety rate).
